# CTAs and conversion

## CTA hierarchy by intent level

| Intent level | CTA |
| --- | --- |
| High | Book a demo · Talk to sales |
| Medium | Get a free competence analysis · See example programme |
| Early | Read guide · Explore resources · View report |
| Existing customer | Contact customer success · Explore programmes |

The primary CTA is normally **Book a demo**. The most important secondary CTA is
**Get a free competence analysis** once the AI analysis tool is live.

On cold-traffic and campaign pages the free analysis may become primary; on warm and
high-intent pages Book a demo stays primary.

## The canonical free-analysis label

The docs use four different labels for this CTA. **These two are canonical:**

| Place | Label |
| --- | --- |
| CTA, anywhere on the page | **Få en gratis kompetanseanalyse** |
| Top-level navigation | **Gratis analyse** |

"Benchmark min bedrift", "Get a free benchmark analysis" and "Free analysis" are all
superseded. `kompetanse` is the word the positioning and the buyer vocabulary are
built on; `benchmark` is English jargon and the tone rules ban jargon.

## Banned CTAs

Never ship a CTA that reads:

`Learn more` · `Submit` · `Download` · `Get started`

Every CTA states the value of the next step. Good: `Book a demo` · `See the platform` ·
`Explore leadership programmes` · `Talk to us about AI training` ·
`Get an example learning path` · `See how it works`.

## One job per page

Each important page has exactly one of each:

- One primary audience
- One main buyer problem
- One primary message
- One primary CTA
- One secondary CTA
- One clear success metric

Plus: proof placed close to claims, and **tracking defined before launch**.

## Demo / contact form

Ask only for what is needed:

Name · Work email · Company · Role · Company size · Area of interest ·
Optional message

The page must state what happens after submission. Example:

> "After you submit the form, we will contact you to understand your competence
> goals and show how Videocation could support your organisation."

## Separate the contact form's jobs

A single generic contact form must not serve sales, support and cancellations at
once. Of 117 submissions on the old site, only ~25–35 were real sales leads — the
sales signal was buried. Route intent to the right destination at the point of
contact, and give enterprise pages a conversion action of their own rather than
letting buyers fall back to `/kontakt`.

## Enterprise pages need their own conversion path

`/bedrift` and `/kompetanseprogram` drew real cross-channel traffic and bounced over
60% because there was nothing to do on them. Any page that earns a visit must offer
a next step.

## A lead is not a meeting

Do not optimise for form submissions. Optimise for qualified conversations and
pipeline. Measure: demo form starts · completions · meetings booked · meetings
attended · qualified opportunities · pipeline · closed-won · conversion by source ·
by page type · by ICP segment.

## Reducing friction

Ask only for necessary information · avoid long forms · make the next step clear ·
allow direct meeting booking where possible · route leads quickly · confirm what
happens after submission · use different CTAs for different intent levels.
