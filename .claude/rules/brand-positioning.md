# Brand and positioning

Who videocation.no is for, and what the site is trying to be. Every page decision
resolves back to this.

## The two jobs

The marketing site has exactly two:

1. Generate qualified B2B leads for sales
2. Build Videocation as a recognised B2B brand

Work that supports neither does not belong on the site.

## Positioning statement

> "We are the competence platform for HR professionals who want to develop
> collective competence — instead of LMS tools built for individual learning."

**Essence:** because we take the complexity out of competence development, it is
easier for companies to build profitable competence in a business landscape that is
changing faster than ever.

**Brand personality:** the leading professional community for corporate learning in
Norway — with heart for our customers. The coach who does not give up until the team
performs, who is measured on results not applause, and who is always honest about
what it takes to succeed.

The site must present Videocation as a **competence development partner, not a
course platform**, and must connect learning to business value.

## Primary audience

Norwegian companies that need systematic competence development for **multiple
employees**. The site speaks to B2B buyers and decision-makers — never to private
individuals or single users.

**Priority roles:** HR · L&D · CEO / General Manager · Business leaders ·
Department managers · Subject matter owners · Training and development managers

**Primary needs:** Leadership development · AI training · HMS training · Sales
development · Project management · Onboarding · Team development · Company-wide
learning initiatives

## Lead definitions

These are load-bearing — reporting and QA both depend on them.

| Term | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| **Qualified B2B lead** | An enquiry from a company where the person has a relevant role, a possible business need, and commercial potential for Videocation |
| **Sales-accepted B2B lead** | A qualified B2B lead that sales confirms is relevant enough to follow up |

**Does not count** towards the lead goal: private individuals · single users ·
support enquiries · existing users asking for help · enquiries with no clear B2B
potential.

## The strategic principle

**The website must reduce buyer uncertainty.**

Every page helps the visitor understand at least one of:

- The problem Videocation solves
- Whether Videocation is relevant for their organisation
- What the customer actually gets
- Why Videocation can be trusted
- What the next step should be

If a page, section or visual does not clarify, prove, guide, or make the brand more
memorable — remove or redesign it.

## What the buyer is really asking

A B2B buyer is not only asking "is this good?" They are asking:

- "Will this work in our organisation?"
- "Will employees actually use it?"
- "Will leadership support it?"
- "Can I defend this purchase internally?"
- "Will this supplier be reliable?"
- "Is the content credible?"
- "Will this create measurable value?"

## Proof, and where it goes

**Place proof next to the claim it supports.** Never collect all proof at the bottom
of the page. Proof appears where doubt appears.

Types available: customer logos · case studies · testimonials · expert profiles ·
metrics · product screenshots · methodology · security/privacy information ·
integration information.

Customer stories use this structure, in order:

Customer · Challenge · Solution · Implementation · Result · Quote · Relevant use case
