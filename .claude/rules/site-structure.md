# Site structure

The site is organised around how B2B buyers think, not around internal product
categories.

## Top-level navigation

In this order:

1. **Solutions**
2. **Platform**
3. **Programmes and content**
4. **Customers**
5. **Resources**
6. **About**
7. **Free analysis**
8. **Book a demo**

**Solutions is the most important section** — most buyers arrive with a business
problem, not a product category in mind.

**Free analysis** stays visible because the AI analysis tool is *expected to become*
an important conversion path. It is not yet a proven one — see
[`cta-and-conversion.md`](cta-and-conversion.md), where it is the most important
**secondary** CTA once the tool is live.

**Book a demo** is the primary high-intent CTA.

## Section purposes

| Section | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Homepage | Position Videocation and route visitors to the right path |
| Solutions | Create relevance for specific buying situations |
| Platform | Explain the product experience and make the solution concrete |
| Programmes and content | Show quality, relevance and breadth of learning content |
| Customers | Reduce risk and prove Videocation can deliver |
| Resources | Build authority, support long buying journeys, create future demand |
| Free analysis | Create value before sales contact, generate buying signals |
| About | Build credibility and explain who Videocation is |
| Book a demo / Contact | Convert high-intent visitors into conversations |

## Per-section CTAs

The free-analysis CTA is **Få en gratis kompetanseanalyse**, and its nav label is
**Gratis analyse**. See [`cta-and-conversion.md`](cta-and-conversion.md) — the other
three labels in the docs are superseded.

Slots below are fixed. English wording is the doc's intent — the shipped label is
Norwegian, and the free-analysis one is fixed as **Få en gratis kompetanseanalyse**.

| Section | Primary CTA | Secondary CTA |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Homepage | Book a demo | Få en gratis kompetanseanalyse |
| Solution pages | Book a demo | Contextual — "See example programme", or the free analysis |
| Platform | See the platform, or Book a demo | — |
| Programmes and content | Explore programmes | Book a demo |
| Customers | See how this could work for your organisation | — |
| Resources | Depends on intent level | — |
| Free analysis | Få en gratis kompetanseanalyse | — |

Resource CTAs by intent: early → **Read guide**, **Explore resources**;
medium → **Få en gratis kompetanseanalyse**; high → **Book a demo**.

## Solution pages

One page per buying situation:

Leadership development · AI competence · HMS and compliance training · Sales
development · Project management · Onboarding · Team development · Strategic
competence development · Learning programmes for distributed organisations

Each explains: the specific buyer problem · why it matters · Videocation's approach ·
what the customer gets · relevant proof · recommended next step.

## The five things buyers must be able to tell apart

Programmes-and-content must make this distinction explicit:

| Component | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Platform | The technology used to deliver and follow up learning |
| Content | Courses, experts, videos, tools and templates |
| Programmes | Structured learning journeys tied to business needs |
| Support | Help with implementation, communication and follow-up |
| Methodology | The principles behind effective competence development |

Differentiation is the *combination*, not the platform alone.

## Priority user journeys

Build and test against these five. Each names its own success indicators.

**1. HR or L&D leader looking for a competence partner**
Homepage → strategic competence development page → methodology → customer proof →
free competence analysis → book a demo

**2. Business leader with a specific competence problem**
Relevant solution page → example programme → expert proof → customer proof →
book a demo

**3. Visitor researching before they are ready to talk to sales**
Resource article or report → related solution page → free competence analysis →
newsletter, report or demo CTA

**4. Existing customer considering expansion**
Programmes and content → solution page → customer proof → contact customer success.
**Must route separately from new B2B prospects.**

**5. Visitor comparing Videocation with LMS alternatives**
Homepage → platform page → methodology → programmes and content → customer proof →
demo

## Resource interlinking

Resources never live in a separate universe. An article about leadership development
links to: the leadership development solution page · a relevant customer case · an
example leadership programme · a demo CTA · a free competence analysis CTA.
