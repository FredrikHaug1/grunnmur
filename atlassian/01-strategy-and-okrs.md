# 01 Strategy and OKRs

## About this document

This document outlines the OKRs (Objectives and Key Results) for [videocation.no](http://videocation.no) in 2026. The OKRs reflect the marketing site's contribution to Videocation's growth in the B2B market.

**Scope of responsibility:** Technical setup, structure, key messaging, landing pages and blog on videocation.no.

**Measurement period:** 2026 (Objective 1 measured from August 2026)

**Data sources:**

* Organic traffic: Matomo (2026 baseline), GA4 from 2027 onwards
* Form data: HubSpot
* Search data: Google Search Console
* Backlinks: Google Search Console

---

## Objective 1: Generate more qualified B2B leads for sales

_Measurement starts August 2026, after new site structure, chat and AI analysis are implemented. Baseline for KR 2 and KR 3 will be established in July 2026 based on early data from the AI analysis tool._

| **Key Result** | **Baseline** | **Target** | **Source** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| KR 1: Number of new B2B prospects from videocation.no in the period August–December 2026 | 0 (new measurement) | 50 | HubSpot |
| KR 2: Share of prospects from companies with 50+ employees | TBD (established July 2026) | TBD | HubSpot |
| KR 3: Number of companies with 50+ employees that reach out after completing an AI analysis, August–December 2026 | TBD (established July 2026) | TBD | HubSpot |

### **Prerequisites**

* Chat implemented before summer 2026
* AI analysis tool (company number lookup with competence gap analysis and programme suggestions) live before summer 2026
* Clear separation between new prospects, existing customers and individual users in HubSpot

---

## Objective 2: Build Videocation as a brand in the B2B market

_All three KRs are measured across the full calendar year 2026._

| **Key Result** | **Baseline** | **Target** | **Source** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| KR 1: Organic traffic to videocation.no | 15,800 clicks (Matomo 2025) | 19,000 clicks | Matomo/GA4 |
| KR 2: Number of unique domains linking to [videocation.no](http://videocation.no) | 89 domains (Search Console May 2026) | 150 domains | Google Search Console |
| KR 3: Organic brand search traffic | 8,100 clicks (Search Console 2025) | 9,700 clicks | Google Search Console |

### **Notes**

* KR 1: Baseline of 15,800 reflects the B2B pivot. Historical figures (2025: 18,200 in Search Console) include B2C traffic that is no longer relevant. Site refactoring may cause a temporary dip in traffic during 2026.
* KR 2: Norsk kompetansekompas (Norwegian Competence Compass) and the blog is the primary driver for new backlinks from professional communities.
* KR 3: Brand search is influenced by site content distributed via LinkedIn and the circulation of Norsk kompetansekompas in relevant professional communities.
