# Marketing Site

## Purpose

This space is the single source of truth for developing [videocation.no](http://videocation.no) as a B2B marketing and sales channel.

The marketing site has two main jobs:

1. Generate qualified B2B leads for sales
2. Build Videocation as a recognized B2B brand

# Current focus

We are improving the marketing site so it attracts the right B2B visitors, explains Videocation’s value clearly and converts relevant visitors into sales-ready enquiries.

# Working principles

* Confluence documents why and what.
* Jira manages who does what and when.
* Decisions should be documented here, not lost in meetings or chat.
* Major Jira work should link back to the relevant Confluence page.
* We prioritise work that supports qualified B2B leads or B2B brand building.
