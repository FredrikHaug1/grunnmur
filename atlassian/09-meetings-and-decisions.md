# 09 Meetings and Decisions

# **1 About this document**

This section defines how meetings, decisions and open questions are documented for the Marketing Site project.

The purpose is to make sure important decisions are visible, easy to find and not lost in, Slack or email.

# **2 Structure**

We use this structure for folders:

`├── Meeting Notes`

`└── Decision Documents`

## **2.1 Meeting Notes**

A folder containing notes from project meetings.

## **2.2 Decision Documents**

A folder containing a decision log and decision documents for decisions that need more explanation than one row in the Decision Log.

### **2.2.1 Decision Log**

A running overview of important decisions.

# **3 Meeting principles**

Meetings should be used to make progress, not to recreate context.

Before a meeting:

* Link relevant Confluence pages
* Add agenda items
* Identify decisions needed
* Identify blockers

During a meeting:

* Capture decisions
* Capture actions
* Assign owners
* Identify unresolved questions

After a meeting:

* Update Jira actions
* Update the Decision Log, if needed
* Link to relevant Confluence pages
* Publish notes within 24 hours

# **4 Meeting types**

## **4.1 Weekly project meeting**

Purpose:

* Review progress
* Prioritize work
* Resolve blockers
* Confirm next actions

## **4.2 Content and messaging review**

Purpose:

* Review page briefs
* Review copy
* Check positioning
* Ensure the page speaks to the target audience

Recommended frequency: As needed

## **4.3 Design review**

Purpose:

* Review page layouts
* Check design system alignment
* Review visual hierarchy and CTA placement

Recommended frequency: As needed

## **4.4 Tracking and performance review**

Purpose:

* Review form data
* Review HubSpot classification
* Review AI analysis tool metrics
* Review traffic and brand indicators

Frequency: Monthly

## **4.5 Launch readiness meeting**

Purpose:

* Confirm launch checklist
* Review open issues
* Decide go/no-go

Frequency: Before major launch

# **7 Decision document template**

Use this format for larger decisions.

### **Decision title**

Use this naming convention:

`Decision - Topic`

Example:

`Decision - Main navigation structure`

### **Decision**

What have we decided?

### **Context**

Why was a decision needed?

### **Options considered**

List the realistic alternatives.

### **Evaluation**

How were the options assessed?

Consider:

* Lead generation impact
* Brand impact
* User experience
* Technical effort
* SEO impact
* Tracking impact
* Risk

### **Final decision**

State the decision clearly.

### **Consequences**

What changes because of this decision?

### **Follow-up actions**

| **Action** | **Owner** | **Due date** | **Jira link** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

### **Date and owner**

Add decision date and owner.

# **8 When a decision document is required**

Create a separate decision document when the decision affects:

* OKRs
* Target audience
* Positioning
* Main navigation
* Sitemap
* Lead definitions
* HubSpot tracking
* AI analysis tool
* Design system
* Launch timing
* Major page structure
* SEO approach
* Technical platform decisions

Small operational decisions can be captured only in meeting notes or the Decision Log.

# **9 Action principle**

Actions should not live only in meeting notes.

If something needs to be done, create or update a Jira task.

Meeting notes should capture the action. Jira should manage delivery.
