# 07 Tracking and Performance

# **1 About this document**

This document defines how we measure the performance of Videocation.no.

The purpose is to understand whether the marketing site is helping Videocation:

1. Generate qualified B2B prospects for sales
2. Build the Videocation brand in the B2B market

The site should not be measured only by traffic or form submissions. We need to measure quality, relevance and contribution to the buying journey.

# **2 Scope**

This document covers tracking and performance measurement for:

* [Videocatio.no](http://Videocatio.no) 
* Landing pages
* Blog and resources
* HubSpot forms
* Chat
* AI analysis tool
* Organic traffic
* Brand search
* Backlinks
* Page-level performance

# **7 Lead source definitions**

We need to separate different types of leads and influence.

| **Source type** | **Definition** |
| --- | --- |
| Site-sourced lead | A lead where videocation.no is the primary source of conversion |
| Campaign-sourced lead | A lead generated from LinkedIn ads, email, webinar, event or campaign traffic |
| Site-influenced lead | A lead where the person visited important pages before converting through another route |
| AI analysis lead | A lead generated through the Company Competence Analysis Tool |
| Chat lead | A lead where the first meaningful contact came through chat |

# **8 Lead type definitions**

| **Lead type** | **Description** | **Counts towards Objective 1?** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| New B2B prospect | New company enquiry with possible commercial potential | Yes |
| Existing customer expansion | Existing customer asking about changes, upgrades or expansion | Supporting metric |
| Single user | Individual user or private person | No |
| Support enquiry | Existing user asking for help | No |
| Other / irrelevant | No clear B2B potential | No |

# **9 Recommended HubSpot fields**

HubSpot should allow us to classify each enquiry clearly.

| **Field** | **Purpose** |
| --- | --- |
| Lead type | Classify the enquiry |
| Source | Identify where the lead came from |
| Landing page | Identify the page that converted |
| CTA | Identify the action taken |
| Interest area | Identify what the person is interested in |
| Company size | Identify whether the company has 50+ employees |
| Organisation number | Connect to company data and AI analysis |
| Domain match status | Check whether email domain matches company |
| Sales status | Track whether sales accepted the lead |
| Outcome | Track whether it became meeting, opportunity, customer or lost |
| MRR impact | Track new or expansion MRR as supporting metric |

# **10 AI analysis tool tracking**

The AI analysis tool should be measured as both a conversion asset and a brand-building experience.

## **10.1 Primary metrics**

* Analysis starts
* Successful company lookups
* Preview-to-email conversion rate
* Full reports sent
* Qualified B2B prospects created
* Companies with 50+ employees identified
* Sales-accepted leads
* Meetings booked
* Opportunities created

## **10.2 Quality metrics**

* ICP fit
* Company size
* Industry relevance
* Domain match rate
* Sales acceptance rate
* Meeting conversion rate

## **10.3 Experience metrics**

* Drop-off at company lookup step
* Drop-off after preview
* Report open rate
* CTA click-through from report
* Return visits from analysed companies

# **11 Page-level performance framework**

Each important page should have one primary job and one main KPI.

| **Page type** | **Primary job** | **Main KPI** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Homepage | Position and route visitors | CTA clicks and solution-page visits |
| Solution page | Create relevance | Demo clicks, AI analysis clicks, scroll depth |
| Platform page | Explain product | Demo clicks, product video views |
| Programme page | Make offer concrete | Programme CTA clicks, demo clicks |
| Customer story | Build trust | Assisted conversions, CTA clicks |
| Resource page | Build authority and early demand | Resource engagement, CTA clicks |
| AI analysis page | Generate qualified prospects | Analysis starts and full reports sent |
| Demo/contact page | Convert demand | Form completion or meeting booking |

# **12 Performance review cadence**

## **12.1 Weekly**

Review operational tracking:

* Form submissions
* Chat enquiries
* AI analysis starts
* Tracking errors
* Broken forms or CTAs

## **12.2 Monthly**

Review marketing site performance:

* Organic traffic
* Brand search
* New B2B prospects
* Sales acceptance
* Top converting pages
* Underperforming pages
* Page-level improvement opportunities

## **12.3 Quarterly**

Review OKR progress:

* Objective 1 progress
* Objective 2 progress
* Lead quality
* Brand growth indicators
* Major site changes required

# **13 Reporting principles**

* Do not report all form submissions as qualified leads.
* Separate new B2B prospects from existing customers, single users and support enquiries.
* Measure both volume and quality.
* Track site influence, not only final conversion.
* Report campaign-sourced leads separately from site-sourced leads.
* Treat customer conversion and MRR as supporting metrics, because sales owns conversion from lead to customer.
