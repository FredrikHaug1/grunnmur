# 02 Target Audience and Positioning

# 1. Target Audience and Positioning

## 1.1 Primary target audience

The primary target audience for videocation.no is Norwegian companies that need systematic competence development for multiple employees.

The site should primarily speak to B2B buyers and decision-makers, not private individuals or single users.

---

## 1.2 Priority roles

The most important roles are:

* HR
* L&D
* CEO / General Manager
* Business leaders
* Department managers
* Subject matter owners
* Training and development managers

---

## 1.3 Primary needs

The target audience may be looking for help with:

* Leadership development
* AI training
* HMS training
* Sales development
* Project management
* Onboarding
* Team development
* Company-wide learning initiatives

---

## 1.4 What counts as a qualified B2B lead?

A qualified B2B lead is an enquiry from a company where the person has:

* A relevant role
* A possible business need
* Commercial potential for Videocation

---

## 1.5 What counts as a sales-accepted B2B lead?

A sales-accepted B2B lead is a qualified B2B lead that sales confirms is relevant enough to follow up.

---

## 1.6 What should not count as a qualified B2B lead?

These should not count towards the main marketing site lead goal:

* Private individuals
* Single users
* Support enquiries
* Existing users asking for help
* Enquiries with no clear B2B potential

---

# 2 Positioning

## **2.1 Essence**

Because we take the complexity out of competence development, it is easier for companies to build profitable competence in a business landscape that is changing faster than ever.

## **2.2 Positive attributes**

With Videocation, anyone responsible for competence development can set up programmes that deliver measurable business value.

## **2.3 Benefits**

I deliver measurable business value and therefore earn a more important and interesting role in the organisation.

## **2.4 Value experience**

Makes me feel like I am good at my job — I develop both my people and the business.

## **2.5 Brand personality**

The leading professional community for corporate learning in Norway — with heart for our customers. We are the coach who does not give up until the team performs, who is measured on results not applause, and who is always honest about what it takes to succeed.

## **2. 6 Positioning statement**  
  
**“**_We are the competence platform for HR professionals who want to develop collective competence — instead of LMS tools built for individual learning._”
