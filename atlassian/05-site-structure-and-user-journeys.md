# 05 Site Structure and User Journeys

# **1 About this document**

This document defines the recommended site structure and priority user journeys for Videocation.no

The purpose is to make sure the marketing site is organised around how B2B buyers think, not around our internal product categories.

The site should help visitors quickly answer four questions:

1. Is this relevant for us?
2. Do they understand our problem?
3. Can they prove that their solution works?
4. What should I do next?

# **2 Strategic principle**

The website must reduce buyer uncertainty.

Every page should help the visitor understand one or more of the following:

* The problem Videocation solves
* Whether Videocation is relevant for their organisation
* What the customer actually gets
* Why Videocation can be trusted
* What the next step should be

If a page, section or visual does not clarify, prove, guide or make the brand more memorable, it should be removed or redesigned.

# **3 Top-level navigation**

The navigation should prioritise B2B buying situations and make it easy for visitors to find the right path.

Recommended top-level navigation:

1. **Solutions**
2. **Platform**
3. **Programmes and content**
4. **Customers**
5. **Resources**
6. **About**
7. **Free analysis**
8. **Book a demo**

## **3.1 Navigation principle**

“Solutions” should be the most important section, because most buyers arrive with a business problem, not a product category in mind.

“Free analysis” should be visible because the AI analysis tool is expected to become an important conversion path before summer 2026.

“Book a demo” should remain the primary high-intent CTA.

# **4 Proposed sitemap**

## **4.1 Homepage**

Purpose: Position Videocation clearly and route visitors to the right path.

Main jobs:

* Explain who Videocation is for
* Make the B2B value proposition clear
* Route users to solutions, programmes, resources or demo
* Introduce the free competence analysis
* Build trust through customers, experts and methodology

Primary CTA: **Book a demo**  
 Secondary CTA: **Get a free competence analysis**

## **4.2 Solutions**

Purpose: Create relevance for specific buying situations.

Recommended solution pages:

* Leadership development
* AI competence
* HMS and compliance training
* Sales development
* Project management
* Onboarding
* Team development
* Strategic competence development
* Learning programmes for distributed organisations

Each solution page should explain:

* The specific buyer problem
* Why the problem matters
* Videocation’s approach
* What the customer gets
* Relevant proof
* Recommended next step

Primary CTA: **Book a demo**  
 Secondary CTA: contextual CTA, for example **See example programme** or **Get a free competence analysis**

## **4.3 Platform**

Purpose: Explain the product experience and make the solution concrete.

Suggested pages or sections:

* How the platform works
* Learning journeys
* Progress tracking and reporting
* Manager follow-up
* Implementation and support
* Integrations, if relevant
* Security and privacy, if relevant for procurement

The platform section should use screenshots, examples and diagrams. It should avoid long abstract explanations.

Primary CTA: **See the platform** or **Book a demo**

## **4.4 Programmes and content**

Purpose: Show the quality, relevance and breadth of Videocation’s learning content.

Suggested pages or sections:

* Competence programmes
* Norwegian experts
* Course library
* Example learning paths
* Templates, tools and reflection exercises
* How content is developed

This section should clearly explain the difference between:

| **Component** | **Meaning** |
| --- | --- |
| Platform | The technology used to deliver and follow up learning |
| Content | Courses, experts, videos, tools and templates |
| Programmes | Structured learning journeys tied to business needs |
| Support | Help with implementation, communication and follow-up |
| Methodology | The principles behind effective competence development |

Primary CTA: **Explore programmes**  
Secondary CTA: **Book a demo**

## **4.5 Customers**

Purpose: Reduce risk and prove that Videocation can deliver.

Suggested pages or sections:

* Customer stories
* Customer logos
* Testimonials
* Use-case examples
* Results and impact
* Industry examples, where relevant

Customer stories should follow this structure:

* Customer
* Challenge
* Solution
* Implementation
* Result
* Quote
* Relevant use case

Primary CTA: **See how this could work for your organisation**

## **4.6 Resources**

Purpose: Build authority, support long buying journeys and create future demand.

Suggested resource types:

* Blog articles
* Strategic guides
* Checklists
* Templates
* Webinars
* Benchmark reports
* Norsk kompetansekompass
* Expert articles
* Tools and analysis pages

Resources should connect naturally to commercial pages.

Example: An article about leadership development should link to:

* Leadership development solution page
* Relevant customer case
* Example leadership programme
* Demo CTA
* Free competence analysis CTA

Primary CTA depends on intent level:

* Early intent: **Read guide**, **Explore resources**
* Medium intent: **Get a free analysis**
* High intent: **Book a demo**

## **4.7 Free analysis**

Purpose: Create value before sales contact and generate stronger buying signals.

This page should present the Company Competence Analysis Tool / AI analysis tool.

The tool should allow visitors to enter an organisation number or company name and receive a company-specific preview before being asked for a work email.

The page should clearly explain:

* What the analysis does
* What data sources are used
* What the visitor gets before email capture
* What is included in the full report
* How the report can help the company identify competence opportunities
* How data is stored and handled
* How sales may follow up

Primary CTA: **Get a free benchmark analysis**

## **4.8 About**

Purpose: Build credibility and explain who Videocation is.

Suggested sections:

* What Videocation believes about competence development
* Why we exist
* The team
* Experts
* Professional community
* Careers, if relevant
* Contact information

The About section should support the brand personality: professional, warm, practical, honest and focused on results.

## **4.9 Book a demo / Contact**

Purpose: Convert high-intent visitors into conversations.

The form or booking flow should be short and clear.

It should ask only for necessary information:

* Name
* Work email
* Company
* Role
* Company size
* Area of interest
* Optional message

The page should explain what happens next after submission.

Example:

“After you submit the form, we will contact you to understand your competence goals and show how Videocation could support your organisation.”

# **5 Priority user journeys**

## **5.1 Journey 1: HR or L&D leader looking for a competence partner**

Entry points:

* Organic search
* LinkedIn post
* Direct visit
* Recommendation

Likely question:

“How can we build competence in a more structured way?”

Recommended journey:

1. Homepage
2. Strategic competence development solution page
3. Methodology section
4. Customer proof
5. Free competence analysis
6. Book a demo

Success indicators:

* Visits solution page
* Starts AI analysis
* Books demo or submits form
* Classified as B2B prospect in HubSpot

## **5.2 Journey 2: Business leader with a specific competence problem**

Entry points:

* Google search
* Shared article
* LinkedIn
* Sales follow-up link

Likely question:

“We need better leadership development / AI competence / HMS training. Can Videocation help?”

Recommended journey:

1. Relevant solution page
2. Example programme
3. Expert proof
4. Customer proof
5. Book a demo

Success indicators:

* Engages with solution page
* Clicks programme example
* Clicks demo CTA
* Becomes sales-accepted B2B lead

## **5.3 Journey 3: Visitor researching before they are ready to talk to sales**

Entry points:

* Blog
* Norsk kompetansekompass
* Webinar
* LinkedIn content

Likely question:

“What should we think about before investing in competence development?”

Recommended journey:

1. Resource article or report
2. Related solution page
3. Free competence analysis
4. Newsletter, report or demo CTA

Success indicators:

* Return visit
* Resource engagement
* AI analysis started
* Later demo request or form submission

## **5.4 Journey 4: Existing customer considering expansion**

Entry points:

* Direct visit
* Email
* Customer success conversation
* Chat

Likely question:

“What else can we use Videocation for?”

Recommended journey:

1. Programmes and content
2. Solution page
3. Customer proof
4. Contact customer success or book meeting

Success indicators:

* Existing customer identified
* Expansion interest registered
* Routed separately from new B2B prospects

## **5.5 Journey 5: Visitor comparing Videocation with LMS alternatives**

Entry points:

* Organic search
* Referral
* Sales conversation

Likely question:

“How is Videocation different from a normal LMS or course library?”

Recommended journey:

1. Homepage
2. Platform page
3. Methodology section
4. Programmes and content
5. Customer proof
6. Demo

Success indicators:

* Visits platform and methodology pages
* Engages with proof
* Books demo
* Sales has clear context for follow-up

# **6 CTA hierarchy**

The site should support different levels of buying intent.

| **Intent level** | **Recommended CTA** |
| --- | --- |
| High intent | Book a demo, Talk to sales |
| Medium intent | Get a free competence analysis, See example programme |
| Early intent | Read guide, Explore resources, View report |
| Existing customer | Contact customer success, Explore programmes |

The primary CTA should normally be **Book a demo**.

The most important secondary CTA should be **Get a free competence analysis** once the AI analysis tool is live.

# **7 Page-level principle**

Each important page should have:

* One primary audience
* One main buyer problem
* One primary message
* One primary CTA
* One secondary CTA
* One clear success metric
* Relevant proof placed close to claims
* Tracking defined before launch
